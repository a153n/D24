Adding a new member

  1. Open your Supabase project.
  2. Go to Authentication → Users → Add user.
  3. Create the member with their email and a temporary password.
  4. Ask them to sign in and change it privately.
  5. Return here and assign their D24 role.

Member accounts are created in Supabase so passwords never pass through this website.

Roles explained

Owner: manages roles, records contributions, and confirms approved projects.

Treasurer: records contributions and confirms approved projects.

Member: views group records and submits/votes on proposals.
